QuizPal
=======

This will be an online game for quizzes.
How users will interact with it:
- a user opens the page and creates a `room`. Use a few letters for identifying the room.
- This initial user will be considered the admin of the room, for as long the room shall exist.
- Then he will share the url for the particular room via any messaging app or via qr code that the others will scan to open that page.
- Each player in the room will register their username.
- When everyone presses ready then they start playing.
- in each user's device (mobile mostly) it will display the question and four answers.
- the players will select their answer.
- when someone selects their answer, it will be displayed to the others that he has selected something but not display their selection yet.
- when everyone has selected an answer then everyone's choice should be revealed and the correct answer as well.
- The players with correct answer will get points, and also the faster a player selected the correct answer the more points he will get.
- The game will have 10 laps. ideally we want this configurable by the admin.
- Gather a large dataset of questions. The questions must be interested and intriguing.
- The questions shall be grouped per category, the categories used in a game shall be configurable by the admin.
- The app and the questions shall be available in english and greek.

Use Reverb for live messaging.
Since this is a simple app use sqlite for database.
